Screening guidance

This is not the jury process itself - it's just a first pass to filter out the productions that have no realistic chance of being nominated, so that juries have at most a couple of hundred productions to consider, rather than thousands.

Do not consider specific categories at this stage - if you think a prod might be interesting enough for any jury to consider further, give it a "yay".

You can afford to be a bit ruthless here: if a prod is "okay" rather than "wow", go ahead and reject it. Ask yourself: can I imagine a universe where a jury is considering this as a potential top five production of the year? If not, do them a favour and save them some work.

Worried that you might be rejecting a hidden treasure? Don't be. There are many more opportunities for a deserving production to make it in front of a jury: we aim for each prod to be screened by two or more people, and just one "yay" is enough to put it through. Then there's the public recommendations, and of course jurors can bring their own suggestions to the table.
